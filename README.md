# Cram

### Smart flashcards for faster, more focused study.

Cram turns study time into short, active sessions. Build decks from the material you already have, use AI to get a head start on a new subject, then review with quick cards that keep you focused on remembering—not just rereading.

[**Start studying with Cram →**](https://foobar404.github.io/deckster/)

## A more active way to learn

Flip through a card to test what you can recall before revealing the answer. Swipe through your deck and rate each response **Again**, **Hard**, **Good**, or **Easy**. Those quick ratings make it simple to record how a session went, while study stats help you see your progress over time.

Prefer to retrieve the exact answer? Use type-to-answer and compare what you entered with the card, including where the spelling differs.

## Create cards your way

- **Start with AI:** Enter a topic, choose an available model, and set how many cards you want to generate. AI-assisted creation is powered through OpenRouter.
- **Bring your own material:** Paste text or import a CSV/TSV file to turn existing notes and lists into cards.
- **Keep it organized:** Group cards into named decks, customize their appearance, and choose what you want to study.

## Designed for getting into a study flow

Cram pairs a clean, modern interface with a quick card-review experience. Its responsive layout works on phones and desktops, so your decks are ready for a focused study session wherever you are.

Whether you are learning a new topic, reviewing class notes, or brushing up on facts, Cram helps you turn spare minutes into useful practice.
